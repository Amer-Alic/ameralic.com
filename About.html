About

The longer version of who I am and what I work on.

Hi, I’m Amer. I’m a software engineer from Zenica, Bosnia and Herzegovina. I build for the web and care about the craft behind useful software: clear thinking, attention to detail, and making good decisions when the answer is not obvious.

This site is where I publish what I am learning about software, work, and the tools changing both. It is intentionally simple: a place for ideas, not a personal brand performance.

What I care about

I am interested in building products that solve real problems, in the difference between producing code and engineering a system, and in how people can keep developing valuable judgment as AI takes over more routine execution.

Elsewhere

You can find my code on GitHub and follow me on X.

Colophon

This site is plain HTML, a small amount of JavaScript, and hand-written CSS. It uses Titan One sparingly for headlines and the system font stack for everything else. No analytics, trackers, or cookie banners.
